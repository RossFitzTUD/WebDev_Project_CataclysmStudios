About Lucas Snow

I’ve always felt most at home in the cold. Ever since I first stepped onto a snowboard as a kid, winter has been my season — the place where everything feels sharper, brighter, and full of possibility. What started as weekend trips to nearby slopes turned into a lifelong obsession with exploring mountains, chasing fresh powder, and finding new trails that push me just a little further each time.


I’m building this website because I want to share that excitement. I want visitors to feel the same rush I get when I’m standing at the top of a mountain, looking down at a trail that’s just waiting to be carved. This space is for my story, for the destinations that inspire me, and for the community of adventurers who make every trip unforgettable.
If you’re here, I hope you feel that spark too. Winter is calling — let’s ride.



My webpage is meant to help people find gear to buy and find out what snowboard spots are the best to grind or carve, I will also include some of my personal tips and tricks on how to get on the level i am!
